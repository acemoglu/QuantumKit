Privacy Policy

Effective 19 August 2026. Covers the QuantumKit app (Mac, iPhone, iPad) and the Swift package.

We do not collect data

QuantumKit does not collect, sell, or share personal information. There is no account, no login, and no QuantumKit server. Circuits run locally on the device. We do not receive your circuits, OpenQASM, histograms, or device identifiers from the app.

Saved circuits stay on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them with the app’s local data.

Network

Simulation does not require a network connection. The app does not phone home.

If you open an App Store, GitHub, or documentation link, that destination has its own policy. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages; GitHub may log requests as any host does. We do not add cookies, ads, or analytics to this site.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page will change. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
